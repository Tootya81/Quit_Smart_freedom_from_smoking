🔁 What is "your loop"? Show ▾Hide ▴

Smoking runs on a repeating loop. A trigger (coffee, stress, a break, your phone) sets off an urge. You smoke, feel brief relief, and your brain learns to link that trigger with a cigarette even more strongly. That's why the same moments keep calling for one.

How this page helps: every craving you log in SOS (and every cigarette you log during prep) records its trigger and time. Below, you'll see your loop: which triggers fire most and when. Once you can see it, you can break it where it's weakest, between the urge and the cigarette, by letting the urge pass.
